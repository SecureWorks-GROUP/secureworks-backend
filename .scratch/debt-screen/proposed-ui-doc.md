# Clear Debt view

Ops > Financials > Clear Debt (`#subCleardebt`, host `#clearDebtRoot`) loads `modules/ops-clear-debt-v2.js` and `.css` through `__swOpsStampAssets`. It is Part B of the CFO desk's debt system review (24 Sep 2026), built to the booking screen's standard ([sales-booking-ui.md](sales-booking-ui.md)): same tokens and card grammar, scoped under `#clearDebtRoot`.

**One read.** Opening the tab makes exactly one request: `GET ops-api?action=debt_worklist&timeline=recent` (contract `debt-worklist/v1`, backend `supabase/functions/ops-api/debt_worklist_read_model.ts`). Choosing a debtor, picking an invoice, filtering the timeline, switching channel and typing a draft all render from that payload with no further request. After that read settles, Refresh starts another. An auth lock or detected operator change clears the cached work list, selection and drafts and invalidates an in-flight result, so the next load reads under the current operator. An unknown-action answer shows one line saying the work list is not connected and that this is not a zero balance; any other failure or a different contract version is one red sentence naming it. A failed read is named instead of being shown as an empty book; a successful read with no debtors says so.

**Read only.** The screen has no write, send, note, proposal-mark or provider call. Its action button is always disabled; the copy says sending or saving a note requires later approval work. Nothing on screen reads as a recorded approval.

The screen is a work list, phone first:

- **Counts line** from `summary` only, with applicable denominators (`n of 101`) in **Details** and the read's `as_of` in the header. **Details** holds the denominator sentences, the fact split (present, missing, no job, unknown), the exactly-once reconciliation, what each source read returned, the read's warnings, and what the read does not carry yet (promise dates, a ready-for-Captain verdict, message links, the send route, and captured fact contents not returned as timeline entries).
- **Debtor list** in the read's order. Search matches the whole query inside one field: any contact name spelling, invoice number, reference, linked job number or ambiguous-link candidate. **Show** filters answer only from fields the read carries (overdue, 60+ days, draft waiting, our message was last, not linked, facts missing, stale or unreadable source, contact not confirmed, dispute, blocked, awaiting allocation, not owed, unclassified). "Stale or unreadable source" covers stale Xero, unreadable invoice events, any debtor fault, and a GHL, email, notes or facts source the read marks `stale` or `unreadable`; **Owner** comes from `owners`. Filters never change the counts line. There is no promise-date or ready-for-Captain filter until the read carries those fields.
- **Debtor card**: name, totals, last contact, name variants on the same contact, the standing-alone reason for an unconfirmed contact, badges for identity, job links, Xero freshness, facts and any unreadable source, the debtor's next step naming its invoice, and each debtor fault with its supplied detail.
- **Invoice picker**: every invoice its own radio row, none picked at first. The draft exists only once one invoice is picked, and is about that invoice only.
- **Timeline**: `debtor.timeline.entries` as one stream, newest first. Entries show their provider, kind, time, source and available invoice or job scope; messages also show direction and a preview, and duplicate message sources are listed when present. Chips filter that same stream. **Captured facts are timeline entries** with `kind: "fact"` (provider `luna`, source `current_job_context_facts`, a `fact` block with `kind`, `value`, `captured_at`, `source_id` and `state` current or stale); nothing else is a fact, and there is no separate facts panel. The Facts chip shows a count only when `sources.facts.timeline_read` is `read`.
  **One rule for empty and partial views** (`timelineLimits` / `absenceLine`): every source behind the current view that was not fully read (GHL unreadable, stale, not read, no job or no contact; email partial, read or current, shown as "Sent emails are not captured yet; that fix is under way." with no owner or fix codes, or stale, unreadable, not read or no job; notes unreadable or not read; Xero stale or invoice events unreadable/not read; facts unreadable, not read, only counted or no job), each capped job and a truncated timeline gets exactly one line under the chips, with the read's owner and fix when it gives them. An absence ("No texts among the 7 stored items read for this debtor.") is said only when every source behind the view was fully read, and only for what was read; beside any limit there is no absence line at all. The card's last-contact line follows the same rule: "Last contact in the stored copies: …", or "Last contact unknown:" followed by the limits. `modules/ops-clear-debt-v2.test.cjs` covers every path in one table.
- **Draft**: Text, Email or Note for the picked invoice. A pending desk proposal of the matching channel loads its exact body and any matching-channel recipient supplied in the read; the card names each route field the read does not supply (phone, sending line, email address, subject, attachment, mailbox). Edits stay in the browser tab, are marked as unsaved, and can go back to the drafted words.
- **Phone**: the list, or the chosen debtor with **All debtors** to go back.
