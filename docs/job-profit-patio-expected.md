# Job Profit: patio expected versus actual

Addition to `docs/job-profit-read-contract.md` (kept apart so it does not collide with open PRs on that file). Rules: `supabase/functions/ops-api/job_profit_patio_expected.ts`. Revision contract consumed: Sales final revision contract, `company-os/sales-final-revision-contract-20261020.md` (final heads: fence `c8d6201`, patio `9c75339`, send gate #1120 `9844f0b`).

`job_profit` (one job) adds `patio_expected` only when the job's last save or its frozen scope revision is a patio scoper 2.0 record, or `{ status: 'unreadable', error }` when either read fails (the profit read itself never fails for it). Every other job's response is unchanged. Only a job with a frozen patio row also reads its whole `scope_json` and `pricing_json` (one job, single-job read), to hash them.

Three sources, never mixed:

| Block | Source | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `current_estimate` (`label: draft_current_estimate`) | `jobs.scope_json`, the last save (four projected paths) | A draft. Shown, never compared with actuals. |
| `frozen_scope` (`label: frozen_scope`) | the job's `scope_revisions` row with `status = 'frozen'`, `tool_kind = 'patio'` (superseded rows are kept but are not the current freeze) | The only expected side compared with actuals. |
| `commercial_baseline` | `v_job_profit` `expected_source` and lanes, untouched; `accepted` is true for `frozen_at_acceptance` and `accepted_quote_revision` | The engine's own baseline, shown beside, never replaced. |

`frozen_scope.server_revision` (`id`, `revision_number`, `frozen_at`, `tool_version`) is the server's row. `tool_revision` (`n`, `frozen_id`, `scope_revision_id`, `parent_revision_id`, `sealed_at`, `outputs_agree`) is the tool's own identity. A `frozenId` is never a server row id, and ids are never used to decide freshness. Freshness is by hash, as the contract requires: the last save's `scope_json` and `pricing_json` are hashed exactly as `freeze_scope` hashes them (`canonicalJsonAndHash`) and compared with the frozen row's `scope_hash` and `pricing_hash`. `scope_hash_match` and `pricing_hash_match` report each; `last_save_matches_frozen` is false when either differs (flag `last_save_not_frozen`), true when both match, and `null` when either could not be compared (flag `last_save_freshness_not_checked`). In every case the frozen row's figures are the ones compared.

Expected figures (ex GST, `materials_ex`, `labour_ex`, `sale_ex`, `cost_ex`, `profit_before_commission_ex`, `margin_before_commission_pct`; the record carries no commission):

- `null` is not known, never 0. Non-numbers and negatives are `null`.
- No figure at all unless the revision is sealed (`n` and `frozenId`) and `outputsAgree` is true.
- No sale unless `supported` is true. `false` or not stated gives no sale.
- No cost, profit or margin while a lane is `null`, or unless the frozen pricing is complete as the contract defines it: `pricing_complete` true, `revision.sealed` true, `internal.costs_complete` not false, and `sell_to_confirm` empty. A missing `pricing_complete` or `revision.sealed` is not a yes (flags `frozen_pricing_complete_not_stated`, `frozen_pricing_seal_not_stated`). A missing `sell_to_confirm` list is not known, never empty: `pricing.sell_to_confirm` is `null` and the pricing reads not complete (flag `frozen_sell_to_confirm_not_stated`).

A frozen revision is not an accepted one (Sales contract, "What a freeze is not"). `frozen_scope.acceptance` (`accepted`, `source`, `scope: 'job'`) is the job's recorded acceptance: a quote document (`job_documents` `type = 'quote'`) with `accepted_at` set, or a `run_acceptances` row with status `accepted`. It is job level, because no record links a frozen revision to the quote that was accepted. Only with `accepted` true do the frozen figures enter `lanes`; otherwise they are still shown in `frozen_scope.expected` but compared with nothing (flag `frozen_not_accepted`, or `frozen_acceptance_not_known` when the evidence could not be read). Evidence found counts even if the other read failed. The two reads (`id` only, `limit(1)`) are made only for a job with a frozen patio row.

`lanes.materials` / `lanes.labour` hold the frozen scope's expected figure beside the engine's actual, plus `actual_complete` and `variance_ex` (actual minus expected). There is a figure only when the frozen scope fixes a cost and the actual lane is complete. Unlinked, ordered-not-billed, emailed-not-placed or open-order materials, or missing or unapproved labour, give no variance, never a false saving. `actual` is the engine's own figures.

An installer bill no recorded person confirmed (JP-12, #1107 `20261020181800`, person rule `20261021173000`) holds both lanes: the engine leaves the whole bill out of actual cost, its labour lines and any materials lines on the same bill alike, and flags `installer_bill_unconfirmed`, so confirmed labour beside it never reads as a complete lane or a saving. An engine without JP-12 never raises that flag, so for a patio job the read asks whether the rule's own function `job_cost_installer_bill` is there (one call with no lines; the rule answers `false`). Only that answer counts as "the flag would be raised": a missing function, an error or any other answer holds both lanes open with `installer_confirmation_not_known`. So this reader must land after #1107; before it, every patio lane reads incomplete.

`sale_matches_sent_quote` compares the frozen sale with `quoted_ex`, within $1. `status` is `complete` only when the frozen cost and profit, both variances and the quote match are all known. Otherwise it is `incomplete`, with `flags` naming why. Flags: `revision_not_frozen`, `frozen_revision_unreadable`, `last_save_not_frozen`, `last_save_freshness_not_checked`, `frozen_pricing_incomplete`, `frozen_pricing_complete_not_stated`, `frozen_pricing_seal_not_stated`, `frozen_costs_incomplete`, `frozen_pricing_not_sealed`, `frozen_sell_to_confirm`, `frozen_sell_to_confirm_not_stated`, `frozen_not_accepted`, `frozen_acceptance_not_known`, `outputs_not_confirmed`, `revision_not_sealed`, `family_not_supported`, `supported_not_stated`, `expected_materials_not_known`, `expected_labour_not_known`, `expected_sale_not_known`, `actual_materials_open`, `actual_labour_open`, `actual_completeness_unreadable` (an unreadable completeness list holds both lanes open), `installer_confirmation_not_known` (the engine cannot flag an unconfirmed installer bill), `no_sent_quote`, `expected_sale_differs_from_sent_quote`, `quote_costs_incomplete` (from #1121 once released).
