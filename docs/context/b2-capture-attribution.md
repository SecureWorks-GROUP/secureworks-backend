# Capture and attribution (B2)

Approved target sections 1 and 2. B1 must apply first. B2 retains SMS bodies, provider message time (separate from write time), provider identity and contact/conversation identity. Current GHL message doors are AGENTS.md "The GHL Webhook Receiver Authenticates First And Logs Ids Only"; the receiver no longer truncates full SMS to 500 characters or uses a conversation as a message identity. Xero invoice auto-link evidence now writes a job UUID. Jarvis owns sms-cache-backfill and must emit the same `ghl:<message id>` identity.

The database ladder binds a direct match only on our own references (job numbers, ACCREC `INV-` numbers, and PO numbers; whole token; at least 5 characters; never a job with `metadata.do_not_schedule`; `20260923230000`), known threads (never for GHL items: a GHL conversation is per contact, not per job), one candidate job, or one candidate job of the line. Since P1a (`20260924140000`) candidates are `context_contact_jobs_at(contact, coalesce(event_at, occurred_at))`: the contact's jobs as they stood at the message time (created by then and not terminal before it, or created later with a lead window covering it), plus contactless jobs matched by the contact's phone or email; a `draft` counts only when no non-draft job is a candidate (since `20260921140000`). Terminal time is when the job last became terminal, not a later archive: earliest `job.status_changed` whose `to` is terminal after the latest non-terminal `to`, then `completed_at`, then `updated_at`. `payload.terminal_time_source='updated_at'` is stamped only when a job dated that way entered the candidate or guard set. One candidate is `single_open` unless another job of the contact became terminal in the 90 days before the message; then the row goes to review with that job listed. A row sent to review stores `candidate_job_ids`, and the Luna guard accepts only a job on that list (or the at-time set when the list is null). `metadata.placement_rule` names the deciding rule. Supplier bill numbers never match. A step-1 hit on more than one of our references returns with no job. Multiple candidates become `pending_luna`; no match stays in `admin_bucket`. Identity is GHL contact id only, never a name. No human queue. Empty/pointer-only and automated rows remain stored but are not extracted. Automated rule inputs are `payload.automated`, `payload.auto_submitted`, existing automated status, and system/audit channels; B5 owns mail-specific rules. The booking-lane hangar is `ensure_booking_draft_job` in `20260921140000`: callable, idempotent on `ghl_contact_id`, no trigger or backfill.

`20260923230000` replaces the whole `resolve_context_attribution` body and writes no existing row; texts already on the holding job stay until a separate logged re-run. Later replacements start with a pre-image guard: `md5(prosrc)` must be a known body or the new one, else they refuse (`context_ladder_preimage_mismatch`). Pin that guard to the body read from production, not the previous repository file; keep the function comment-free so the hash does not depend on how the body was applied; update the guard hashes, the contract case's `production-preimage.sql`, and the rollback together. Rollback restores the pre-L1 body byte for byte. Contract: `supabase/tests/migration-contracts/20260923230000_context_ladder_step1_own_references_live`. P1a (`20260924140000`) is the next replacement; its guard, rollback (restores the L1, 20260914110000 re-run and both live Luna bodies byte for byte) and named-row contract: `supabase/tests/migration-contracts/20260924140000_context_placement_at_time`.

Service-role APIs:

- `context_attribution_jobs(p_contact_id text)` returns id, job_number, type, status, site_suburb, updated_at.
- `attribute_context_event_with_luna(p_event_id uuid,p_job_id uuid,p_confidence numeric)` atomically locks a pending row. It binds a thread only for non-GHL items; a GHL conversation is never a job thread. Null job means bucket. An existing racing thread winner is preserved and returned. Missing/not-pending/disabled/invalid candidate calls fail explicitly. The four-argument outcome overload and attempt record: `docs/context/a1-attribution-attempts.md`.
- `rerun_context_attribution(p_limit integer=250,p_contact_id text=null)` returns rows reconsidered. Oldest checked rows rotate first, max 1000. Since `20260924140000` it selects only rows with no job (L1b's re-run line) and persists `candidate_job_ids`. Since P1b (`20260924160000`) a job insert no longer calls it: the trigger returns at once for a job with no GHL contact or a holding job, otherwise it calls `context_reconsider_contact(contact, since, 'job_created', job)` with `since` = the new job's lead-window start. For each of the contact's messages from `since` up to the job's creation for which the new job is an at-time candidate: a bucket row re-runs the ladder; an `unplaced` row, or one placed on a sibling by `single_open`, `single_line` or `luna`, goes back to `pending_luna` with the old job, the new job and every at-time candidate stored (the 774 line does not decide it); a `pending_luna` row gains the new job as a candidate. `direct`, `thread`, `content_ref` and `party` rows never move, nor a non-GHL row whose conversation is bound in `event_threads`. A row whose stored candidates already name the job, or already reconsidered for it, is left alone (once per event); the same test is applied again under the row lock. Moved rows carry `metadata.capture_mode='relink'` (prior value in `capture_mode_before`) and `metadata.placement_reconsidered`; they never wake extraction on their own. A row another session holds is skipped and counted (never waited on); at most 500 rows and 2 s of work per call, newest first; the job insert never fails on it. The daily worker runs remaining rows; ambiguous rows become pending Luna.

K1 (`20260924030000`) owns `context_extraction_candidates` and
`context_extraction_events` (due rule, live wake, batch order). Do not restate
them here. Holding-job extractability stays `context_job_extractable` in

Historical rows are deterministically classified in 250-row batches without assigning `context_captured_at`. No extraction backfill, model call, cron or production action is performed by B2.

Rollback removes triggers and callable code while preserving captured rows and thread custody. It does not reverse historic attribution or delete evidence. Restore prior endpoint code with the rollback; the retained additive columns keep those writes compatible.

Undated provider evidence retains a null `event_at`; attribution never substitutes ingestion time. Internal event producers likewise supply their occurrence date explicitly. Persist source time is owned by `docs/context/b3-fact-custody.md`.

### Remaining capture coverage and mailbox compatibility

Bounded writer inspection still finds gaps: `addPhoto`/`attachJobPhoto` and Trade photo upload persist `job_media` plus `job_events` without retained business-event document/media pointers; work-order creation emits a metadata-only `wo.created`, while `sendWorkOrder` marks sent and logs only `job_events`. Document assembly/attachment writers similarly need producer-by-producer bridging. These paths are not fixed by note capture and are not capture-all acceptance.

Recommended follow-up: after each successful authoritative row write, create a deterministic source-table/source-ID envelope with that row's date and explicit job, original MIME/hash and a private evidence copy/pointer. Retain pointer-only rows without pretending they contain readable/model-extractable text. Keep each writer's operational file/URL intact; do not convert existing public buckets or remove business files. No bulk historic replay is implied.

B5's safe compatibility seam is after durable normalized-mail capture and `inbox_events` projection: a separate deterministic, idempotent supplier projection can restore classification/priority/action fields using sender/subject/explicit reference rules, then file only after authoritative attribution. A private document access route/short-lived signed-link reader is needed before projecting private attachment pointers into current `job_documents`/`job_media` consumers, which expect public URLs. Do not restore paid classification or publish private capture bytes to make the old UI work. Existing SES operational intake remains separate and active. This seam is proposed; the removed supplier filing/classification behavior remains a B5 compatibility gap until implemented and tested.
