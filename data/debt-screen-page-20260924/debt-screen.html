04 · The six-part build

From a clear list to an agentic follow-up

One job per step. The engine proposes exact words. The Captain approves those words. The executor checks again when pressed, then sends once.

01Merged and live

Keep automatic sending shut

We keep the chaser guarded. Correct means a disabled or unreadable flag produces zero provider calls.

The merged Jarvis change is live: https://github.com/SecureWorks-GROUP/secureworks-jarvis/pull/194
Then we move on.
02With CIO

Bring the missing evidence in

We capture email and repair message capture freshness. Correct means each captured message lands once with its source and time.

Promises and disputes stay unknown until email capture and MN1 land. The evidence pack links the open MN1 work: https://github.com/SecureWorks-GROUP/secureworks-backend/pull/914
Then we move on.
03In validation

List every open invoice once

We show all 101 baseline invoice IDs, including 11 without a job link. Correct means zero missing and zero duplicates.

Each row shows its source, freshness and link state. A failed read stays visible as a fault.
Then we move on.
04Green, awaiting step 03

Make the screen easy to judge

We keep the eight colored tiles and add an explicit invoice choice and one evidence timeline. Correct means zero sends when opening or reading the screen.

Screen review: https://github.com/SecureWorks-GROUP/secureworks-ux/pull/349. Recommendation: restore the bar and tiles above its work list.
Then we move on.
05Dry run · execute off

Approve exact words, then send once

The engine proposes a message for a named invoice and recipient. The Captain approves it. At the press, the executor checks balance, recipient and exact words again.

Correct means any changed fact refuses, and a retry cannot send a second message. Real execution remains off during validation.
Then we move on.
06Merged and live

Leave the scheduled chaser stopped

We keep automation off. Correct means scheduled debt-chase sends stay at zero and a run produces only a ranked proposal or an auditable no-op.

The Jarvis schedule guard and stopped sending path are live: https://github.com/SecureWorks-GROUP/secureworks-jarvis/pull/196
Then we move on.

Do not let a blank pane look like an answer.

The read on 24 September at 01:23 AWST found 101 open receivables. Promises and disputes remain unknown until email capture and MN1 land. Some current message sources are also not fresh. A coloured category is a useful desk judgement, not proof that the source record is complete.

Correct means: every missing, stale or unreadable source is named on the debtor. The screen never invents a promise, a dispute result or a clean zero.
06 · What happens before a send

Three checks around one exact message

The design makes authority and current facts visible in the screen, not implied by a browser confirmation.

1 · Engine proposesExact message, exact recipient, exact invoice, and the Xero facts it used.Nothing leaves the screen.
2 · Captain approvesHe sees the exact words and the invoice they refer to.Approval is bound to those facts.
3 · Executor checks and sends onceAt the press, re-read the balance and recipient. Refuse if anything moved.A retry cannot duplicate a confirmed send.
Captain: look at the screen first.Then say what to change. The current colours are kept in this recommendation; any further change is yours to call.
