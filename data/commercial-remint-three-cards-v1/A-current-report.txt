    Work Order Scope

Attend to make safe to the partially collapsing ceiling.


    Site Findings

Storm and wind damage left the ends of the roof tiles cracked with large gaps open between the tiles, allowing water into the
roof space above the ceiling and causing the ceiling below to begin to fail.


    Works Completed

We siliconed and flashing taped the cracked tile ends and the open gaps between the tiles to stop further water entering the
roof space above the ceiling.


    Materials and Equipment

Silicone x 1
Flashing tape x 1
