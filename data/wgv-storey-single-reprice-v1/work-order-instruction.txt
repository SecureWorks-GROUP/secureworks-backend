The contradicting work-order statement, read from production jobs.notes for
SWMS-261114 (the same field the intake storey matcher reads). Client name, street
address and phone are not present in the extract below.

  "Allocation Work Order MLB-RR-26836PO-57514 Roof Reports EXTERNAL
   PLEASE ATTEND SITE TO COMPLETE THE FOLLOWING: Roof Report Request
   Please attend site and conduct a two storey roof report noting cause of
   damage/point of water entry, along with any maintenance issues identified.
   If multiple stories noted, please contact the office ..."

`roofStoreyOrderedProductFact` (makesafe_roof_storey_fact.ts) matches
"two storey roof report" against its ordered-product pattern and yields
storeys = "double". That is how jobs.metadata.storeys = "double" was recorded at
intake, and it is the value U4 still prices from today.
