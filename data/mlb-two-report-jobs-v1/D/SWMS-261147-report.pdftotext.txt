    Work Order Scope

Make safe the smashed bedroom window and roller door.


    Site Findings

Fire. Completely smashed bedroom window and smashed roller door around the handle. Glass shattered around the window
area.


    Works Completed

Cleaned up glass shards and removed glass still in the bedroom window frame. Taped any sharp edges on the roller door
and removed any loose glass. Framed both the bedroom window and sliding door, then screwed plyboard onto the frame
creating a secure board-up.


    Materials and Equipment

Structural timber x 8m
Plyboard 12mm 2.4x1.8 x 3
Screws x 40
